How this works

First drag the blue square handles so the box fits around the card, keeping the background out but leaving a little card margin. The grid is then found inside the box. Each circle shows the area that is averaged for that cell (green = punched, red ring = not); resize it with Sample area. If the grid is off, slide it with the arrows or drag the orange handles (TL, TR, BR, BL) onto the corner cells. Your manual changes stay until you move the box or press Find grid again. The number of rows is taken from the photo; zoom in to check the grid.

How this works

Turn any picture into a punch card. Each cell takes the average brightness of the part of the picture it covers, so fine detail blends instead of being skipped. Dark areas become holes. Dithering then guesses the in-between greys by mixing holes and blanks.

How this works

Compare with your real card and tap or drag cells to fix them; the preview on the right updates as you edit. Arrow keys shift the whole card (it wraps around). To repeat a motif, draw it inside the dashed amber box at the top-left, set its size, pick a layout and press Repeat motif. Undo or Ctrl+Z steps back. In the preview, holes use colour B; the thick outline marks your card once and dashed lines show where it repeats.
