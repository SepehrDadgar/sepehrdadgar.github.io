# Punchcard Studio

Punchcard Studio turns punch cards for Brother-style knitting machines into digital patterns, and turns any picture into a new card. It also helps you knit from them without losing your place.

## What you can do

- **Scan card:** photograph a card, draw a box around it, and the 24-column grid is found automatically. Zoom in to check it and slide it if it's off by a cell.
- **From picture:** pick any image and it's turned into a card. Each cell averages the part of the picture it covers, and dithering fakes the in-between greys.
- **Check & fix:** tap cells to correct mistakes, add or remove rows, and repeat a motif as straight, brick, half-drop or mirrored. A live fabric preview sits beside the card.
- **Knit:** the current row is highlighted on the card, with the punched column numbers listed below. Move through the rows with the buttons or arrow keys, and your place is saved in the browser.

Everything runs in your browser, so your photos are never uploaded. The card is saved on your device, and you can export it as a file to keep or share.
